## Timing

- First 30 minutes determines reach
- Best: 9 AM to 12 PM weekdays
- Peak days: Wednesday, Friday
- Post frequency: 3-5 quality daily, spaced 2-3 hours

## What Kills Reach

1. External links (non-Premium): 0% engagement
2. 3+ hashtags: engagement drops
3. No visual element: 5x penalty vs video
4. Text-only: lowest format
5. Slow first-30-min engagement

## Thread Best Practices

- First tweet: no number, use 🧵
- Number from tweet 2: "2/7", "3/7"
- Each tweet under 200 chars (250 max)
- Line breaks between ideas
- 1-2 hashtags, final tweet only
- Links in final tweet or reply
- Optimal: 7 tweets (5-10 range)
- 8-12 tweets perform 47% better than shorter

## Article Requirements

- Minimum 1,000 words for prize eligibility
- X Premium required (any tier)
- Original content only
- No supporting tweet needed (distributes on its own)
- Include hero image
- Higher dwell time = algorithm reward
